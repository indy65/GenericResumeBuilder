FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeBuilder really free?
Yes — completely free. There are no paid tiers, no watermarks on exports, and no premium templates hidden behind a paywall. This landing page is ad-supported, but the builder itself is entirely ad-free.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Your resume data is saved automatically to your browser's local storage. As long as you use the same browser, your work will be there when you return — no login required.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Your resume data never leaves your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you want to back up your data or transfer it to another device, use the Export JSON feature in the builder.
Can I use this for non-tech jobs?
Absolutely — that's exactly what it's built for. You create your own skill categories to match your profession. Whether you're a nurse, lawyer, designer, or teacher, the builder adapts to you.
Will my resume pass ATS systems?
The "ATS Professional" template uses clean single-column formatting that parses well in most applicant tracking systems. No resume builder can guarantee compatibility with every ATS, but this template gives you the best chance.
Can I create multiple resumes?
Yes. Use the "My Resumes" button in the builder to create, name, duplicate, and switch between multiple resume versions — useful for tailoring your resume to different roles or industries.
What export formats are supported?
You can export your resume as a PDF (via browser print) or as a Word document (.docx) with one click. Both formats are ready to attach to a job application.
