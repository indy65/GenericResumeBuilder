Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🔒 The short version: ResumeBuilder does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Your resume stays on your device.

1. Overview

ResumeBuilder ("we", "our", or "the service") is a free, browser-based resume builder. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and builder tool.

We are committed to your privacy. Our builder is designed from the ground up to store your data locally on your own device — not on our servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The resume builder tool does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal contact information
  • Your work history, education, or skills
  • Your profile photo or any uploaded images
  • Your resume content in any form

All resume data you enter is saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage — a storage mechanism built into your browser that never sends data to any external server.

3. Local Storage

ResumeBuilder uses your browser's localStorage to automatically save your resume data between sessions. This data:

  • Exists only on your device and in your browser
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data or using the "Clear" button in the builder
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party

4. Google AdSense

Our landing page (index.html) displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. When you visit the landing page, Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing history. This is separate from the resume builder itself.

Google's advertising practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

The resume builder (builder.html) does not display advertisements and does not load any Google AdSense scripts.

5. Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand basic traffic patterns like page views and referral sources. This data is aggregated and does not include personally identifiable information.

No analytics scripts are loaded inside the resume builder tool itself.

6. Cookies

The resume builder does not use cookies. The landing page may receive cookies from Google AdSense. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services

ResumeBuilder is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect basic server-level logs (IP addresses, request metadata) as part of standard hosting infrastructure. These logs are governed by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

We use Google Fonts for typography on the landing page. This involves a request to Google's servers to load the font files. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

8. Children's Privacy

ResumeBuilder is intended for adults seeking employment. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our service, please contact us.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not collect your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, correct, or export on your behalf. To remove your resume data, simply clear your browser's localStorage or use the "Clear" button in the builder.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of ResumeBuilder after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out via the contact information on our website.
